### Featured Projects

#### [OnAir](https://chaiovercode.com/onair)
> **Native macOS menu bar app** that keeps your meetings always visible. Focus timer, auto-join, conflict detection, natural language event creation, stats dashboard. 2MB, no Electron.
>
> *Stack:* Swift, SwiftUI, EventKit

#### [OverGraph](https://overgraph.in)
> **IPL match visualizer** with Elo ratings, live win-probability from 10K Monte Carlo simulations per over, and over-by-over scoring breakdowns.
>
> *Stack:* React, TypeScript, Monte Carlo, Elo

#### [Narrativ](https://chaiovercode.com/narrativ)
> **AI-powered visual storytelling app.** Research any topic, generate beautiful image carousels, and export for social media. Local-first with iCloud sync.
>
> *Stack:* Tauri, React, Python, Gemini API

#### [GapTrack](https://chaiovercode.github.io/gaptrack/#/)
> **Personal progress tracker** that helps you bridge the gap between where you are and where you want to be. Track goals, habits, and growth metrics with beautiful visualizations.
>
> *Stack:* React, TypeScript, Tailwind, Charts

#### [AnnotateThis](https://github.com/chaiovercode/AnnotateThis)
> **PDF annotation tool powered by Gemini.** Upload a document and it auto-highlights key insights. Making dense PDFs easier to digest.
>
> *Stack:* TypeScript, Gemini API, PDF.js

#### [Stock Arena](https://github.com/chaiovercode/stockarena)
> **Stock analysis using multiple AI agents.** Three agents debate — bull, bear, and judge — to analyze stocks from different angles. Exploring CrewAI patterns.
>
> *Stack:* CrewAI, LangGraph, Python

#### [AQI Map](https://github.com/chaiovercode/ai-engineering/blob/main/apps/aqi-cards/aqi.py)
> **Generative art engine for air quality.** Paints your city's air quality in real-time using satellite data to render 3D miniatures — smoggy for Delhi, clear for Bangalore.
>
> *Stack:* Python, Gemini

#### [AI Leadership Notes](https://chaiovercode.github.io/ai-leadership-notes/)
> **Open-source curriculum** bridging the gap between technical AI foundations and business strategy. Frameworks, case studies, and notes on leading AI initiatives.
>
> *Stack:* HTML, GitHub Actions, Claude Code
